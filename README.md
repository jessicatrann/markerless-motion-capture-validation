# Markerless Motion Capture Validation

## Overview
This research project evaluated the reliability of a markerless motion capture system (**Theia3D**) under different clothing conditions, using quantitative comparisons of joint kinematics.

## Study
- **28 typically developing children**, ages 4–18.
- Motion-analysis outputs processed with **Visual3D** and **MATLAB**.
- Knee, ankle, and hip kinematics compared using root mean square deviation (**RMSD**).

## Analysis workflow
1. Export relevant kinematic signals from Visual3D.
2. Organize participant and clothing-condition trials.
3. Align and compare corresponding joint-angle time series.
4. Calculate RMSD and summarize differences by side and condition.
5. Prepare plots and summaries for publications and research presentation.

## Reported findings
Across experimental conditions, the largest RMSD values were seen in the sagittal-plane reported knee deviations of **7.2° (left)** and **6.8° (right)**, the frontal
plane reported hip deviations of hips **-3.3° (left)** and **3.8° (right)**, and the transverse plane reported ankle deviations of **3.5° (left)** and **3.8° (right)**.
